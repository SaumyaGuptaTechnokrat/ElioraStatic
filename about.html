Our Story

Named for light. Built to deliver it.

Eliora means "God is light" — and that idea shapes everything we make, from a single 9W bulb to industrial fittings that light up entire warehouses.

Why We Exist

Light shouldn't be complicated.

Too many lighting choices force a trade-off — brightness for efficiency, style for durability, price for quality. Eliora was founded to remove that trade-off entirely.

Every product that carries our name goes through the same question: does this make light simpler, safer and better for the person using it? If the answer is yes, it earns the Eliora badge.
